Before you're born

Where will you start?

You can't choose your family. Your country sets your wages, rent and house prices. After that, every big decision is yours.

Difficulty
The original, gentler game: you see projected results for every option before you choose. Like real life: more surprise bills, rising costs, jobs and degrees that sometimes don’t pay off, and trade-offs between money and the rest of life. You see prices, pay and fees, not results; after you choose, a hindsight card shows how your choice turned out. Harsher still: more shocks, higher taxes, deeper crashes and longer slumps. No hints at all; every decision is revealed on your end card.
Big moment cards
A card pops up and time stops for big moments, like a new job, a wedding or a layoff. Turn it off to see them as small notes instead.
Pace
Time stops at every decision. You can pause or skip ahead.
