Drag the age to watch the character grow up: school, university, a career, a partner, children, a home, retirement.

Ages

Baby, child, teen, adult and elder. The green sprout marks you (partners and children don't have one).

Expressions

Happy is ^ ^, normal has sparkly eyes, sad eyes are teary, a serious illness makes the eyes dizzy, old age gets sleepy. The last one is the end card. Blinking happens by itself every few seconds.

Emotes

Hearts and sparkles float up at big moments; the sweat drop, zzz and rain cloud stay while that's true.

Outfits

All 31 careers, then studying, school, the hospital, recovering, and everyday clothes when poor, comfortable or rich.

Family
